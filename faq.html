About HobbyMatch

What is HobbyMatch?

HobbyMatch® is a free UK app that matches you with hobbies that suit you, the people who share them, and local clubs, classes and events. More about HobbyMatch.

Is HobbyMatch free?

Yes. HobbyMatch is free to download on the App Store. The Android version is coming soon.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. HobbyMatch is on iPhone now, and the Android version is coming soon.

Who can use HobbyMatch?

Anyone aged 13 or over.

Finding things to do and people

How does it find things near me?

You tell it how far you'll travel. HobbyMatch then shows venues, clubs, classes, events and sessions inside that distance, using your phone's location if you allow it.

Can other people see where I am?

Other people see a general area or a distance, not your exact location. You can also make your profile private in Account, then Private profile. Our privacy policy has the details.

Do I need someone to go with?

No. Join a session another hobbyist has posted, follow people near you who do the same thing, or go on your own.

How do leagues work?

Start a league for anything you play against someone, then invite people with a link. You log a score, the other side confirms it, and the table updates. Run it as a season or keep a rolling table.

Clubs and venues

How do I list my club, studio or venue?

Download HobbyMatch, find your club, studio or venue and claim it. It's free. If it isn't there yet, email support@hobbymatch.fit and we'll add it. See HobbyMatch for business.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account, then Delete my account. Your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days. If you can't sign in, email support@hobbymatch.fit from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.
