About HobbyMatch

HobbyMatch® is a free UK app that matches you with hobbies that suit you, the people who share them, and local clubs, classes and events.

What HobbyMatch does

  • Hobby discovery. Answer four quick questions (the pace you want, what you'd spend, whether you want company and how far you'll travel) and HobbyMatch suggests hobbies that fit, each with the reason it suits you.
  • What it costs. Each suggestion comes with a rough UK cost, so you know what you're getting into before you buy any kit.
  • Local places. Clubs, classes and venues inside the distance you chose.
  • What's on this weekend. Events and sessions near you for the hobbies you've picked. See what's on this weekend.
  • People who share your hobbies. Join sessions other people nearby have posted, post your own, or follow people who do the same things.
  • Leagues. Start a league for anything you play against someone. Both sides confirm each result before it counts.
  • Keeping it going. Log what you did, build a weekly streak and get cheered on by the people who follow you.

HobbyMatch is free on iPhone. The Android version is coming soon. It's for anyone aged 13 or over.

For clubs, studios and venues

Clubs and venues can list themselves for free, add their sessions and reach local people looking for their hobby. See HobbyMatch for business.

Where our listings come from

Events and venues come from clubs and venues that list themselves on HobbyMatch, from public club calendars, from OpenActive open data published by leisure operators (each listing credits its publisher), and from Ticketmaster for ticketed events.

Your privacy

Other people see a general area or a distance, never your exact location. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and why.
