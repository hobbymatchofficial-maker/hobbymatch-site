Support

Something not working, or a question about your account? Email us and a person will reply, usually within 24 hours.

Email

support@hobbymatch.fit

Delete your account

In the app, go to Account, then Delete my account. Your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.

Reset your password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you. Open the link on the same phone you asked from.

Report someone

Open their profile, tap the ⋯ menu, then Report. You can block them from the same menu.

Your data

Our privacy policy explains what we collect and why.
