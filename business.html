Why list

More of the right people through the door

Most people who'd love your club have never heard of it. HobbyMatch puts you in front of them at the moment they're deciding what to try.

  • It's free. No fees to claim your venue or list what you run.
  • Local people, your hobby. People tell HobbyMatch what they're into and how far they'll travel, so you reach those nearby who want what you offer.
  • Your timetable, kept current. Add sessions and events yourself, or connect your calendar feed and let it update on its own.
  • See who's coming. Find out who's going to each session, so you know who to say hello to.
  • Know it's working. See how many people view your venue and follow you.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Claiming your venue and listing your sessions and events on HobbyMatch is free.

How do I claim my venue?

Download the HobbyMatch app, find your venue and claim it. If you can't find it, or someone else needs to manage it, email support@hobbymatch.fit and we'll sort it out.

Do I have to type in every session by hand?

No. You can add sessions and events one by one, or connect your calendar feed so your timetable stays up to date on its own.

What kinds of places can list?

Local clubs, societies, studios, classes, gyms and venues for any hobby, from padel and climbing to choirs, chess and pottery. If people can turn up and take part, it belongs on HobbyMatch.

We're tiny and run by volunteers. Is it worth it?

Small clubs are exactly who we built this for. Claiming takes a few minutes, there's nothing to pay, and it's one more way for new members to find you without anyone putting up posters.
