From not sure what to try, to doing it every week

  1. Answer four questions

    Pace, budget, whether you want company, and how far you'll go. About 90 seconds, and nothing else.

  2. See hobbies that fit

    Every match says why it suits you, what it costs, and how many places to do it are nearby.

  3. Go along

    Join a session another hobbyist has posted, find a club or venue, or start on your own.

  4. Keep it going

    Log what you did in a few taps, build a weekly streak, and play people in a league.

Matches that say why they fit

No percentages to decode. A strong match is one that fits all four of your answers, and the card tells you what it's like and what's within reach.

  • Real places nearby. Venues, clubs and classes inside the distance you chose.
  • What's on this weekend. Events and sessions for your hobbies, with the hobby named on each one.
  • People who do it too. Follow hobbyists near you and plan a first session together.

Play people nearby and keep a table

Start a league for padel, tennis, chess, football or anything you play against someone. One on one or in teams, invite only or open to people near you.

  • Results both sides agree on. You log the score, they confirm it, and only then does it count.
  • Invite anyone with a link. Friends who don't have HobbyMatch yet can join after a quick download.
  • Seasons or ongoing. Run it for eight weeks and crown a winner, or keep a rolling table.
