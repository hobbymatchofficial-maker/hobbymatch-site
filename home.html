Questions

Is HobbyMatch free?

Yes. HobbyMatch is free to download on the App Store and Google Play.

How does it find things near me?

You tell it how far you'll travel. HobbyMatch then shows venues, clubs, classes, events and sessions inside that distance, using your phone's location if you allow it.

Can other people see where I am?

Other people see a general area or a distance, not your exact location. You can also make your profile private in Account, then Private profile. Our privacy policy has the details.

Do I need someone to go with?

No. Join a session another hobbyist has posted, follow people near you who do the same thing, or go on your own.

How do leagues work?

Start a league for anything you play against someone, then invite people with a link. You log a score, the other side confirms it, and the table updates. Run it as a season or keep a rolling table.

Who can use HobbyMatch?

Anyone aged 18 or over. If you need help with your account, see Support.
