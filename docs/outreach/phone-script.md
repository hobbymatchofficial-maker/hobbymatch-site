# 30-second phone script

For calling a club, studio or venue, or chatting at the front desk. About 30 seconds spoken. Sound like yourself, not a script.

---

**Opening (5 seconds)**
> Hi, is that [Club name]? I'm [your name] from HobbyMatch. Have you got 30 seconds? It's nothing to buy.

*If they're busy:* "No worries, when's better? Or I can just email you." Get the email address and use the cold email.

**The point (15 seconds)**
> We're a free app that helps people round here find a hobby and somewhere local to do it. People tell it what they're into and how far they'll travel, and it shows them clubs like yours.
>
> You can claim [Club name] in the app for free, add your sessions or link your calendar, and see who's coming and how many people are looking at you.

**The ask (10 seconds)**
> Would it be all right if I emailed you the link? It takes a few minutes to set up.

*If yes:* confirm the email address and send the cold email straight after, mentioning the call ("Lovely to speak to you just now").
*If no:* "That's absolutely fine. Thanks for your time, and good luck with the club."

---

## If they ask...

**"What does it cost?"**
Nothing. Claiming your venue and listing your sessions is free.

**"Who are you?"**
HobbyMatch Ltd, a UK company. The app's on the App Store and Google Play, and the website is hobbymatch.fit.

**"We've already got a website / Facebook page."**
Great, keep it. This is about reaching people who don't know you exist yet and are looking for something like you.

**"We're full."**
Fair enough. You might still want to claim it so the details people see are right, and for when you've got space again.

**"Who's 'going'? Do I see their details?"**
You'll see who's said they're going to your sessions in the app. For anything about people's data, point them to hobbymatch.fit/privacy or support@hobbymatch.fit.
