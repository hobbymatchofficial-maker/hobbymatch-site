hobbymatch

For clubs, studios and venues

Help new people find your club. Free.

HobbyMatch is a free app that helps people nearby find a hobby and somewhere local to do it. Put your club in front of them.

  • Free to list. Claim your venue in the app. Nothing to pay.
  • Add what's on. Your sessions and events, or connect your calendar feed.
  • See who's coming. Who's going, and how many people view and follow you.
  • Reach local people. Folk nearby looking for exactly your hobby.

Claim your venue in a few minutes

  1. Download HobbyMatch
  2. Find your club and claim it
  3. Add your sessions
