# Outreach kit for clubs and venues

Internal material for getting local clubs, studios and venues to claim their venue on HobbyMatch. Nothing here is linked from the website.

| File | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [cold-email.md](cold-email.md) | First email to a club you haven't spoken to |
| [follow-up-email.md](follow-up-email.md) | One gentle nudge, about a week later |
| [phone-script.md](phone-script.md) | 30 seconds on the phone or at the front desk |
| [flyer-a5.html](flyer-a5.html) | A5 flyer for club noticeboards. Open it in a browser and print |

## A few ground rules

- **Write like a neighbour, not a salesperson.** These are mostly volunteer-run clubs. Keep it short, warm and easy to say no to.
- **Personalise one line.** Mention something real about their club (their Tuesday beginners' night, the new courts, last weekend's open day). It's the difference between a reply and the bin.
- **Only one follow-up.** If they don't reply after that, leave it.
- **Don't promise what the app doesn't do.** Stick to what's in these templates: it's free, they claim their venue in the app, add sessions or connect a calendar feed, and see who's going, views and followers.
- **Send from a real person's name**, and use support@hobbymatch.fit as the reply address if you're not monitoring your own inbox.

## Printing the flyer

1. Open `flyer-a5.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
2. Print, set paper size to **A5** (or A4 and "fit to page" if you only have A4), margins **None**, and turn on **Background graphics**.
3. The QR code goes to https://hobbymatch.fit/business.

The flyer also prints fine in black and white.
